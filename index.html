Welcome to ShoeMate

ShoeMate is the first location-based app designed specifically for sneaker and shoe collectors. Whether you're hunting for rare kicks, looking to trade, or want to connect with collectors who share your passion — ShoeMate makes it easy.

But we're more than just a marketplace. We solve a unique problem: people with different shoe sizes for each foot. If you wear a size 10 on your left foot and size 11 on your right, finding that perfect mismatched pair has never been easier.

How It Works

  • Create your profile with your shoe sizes (including split sizes!)
  • Swipe through collectors in your area
  • Match with people who have complementary sizes
  • Chat and arrange trades or purchases
  • Meet safely in public and complete your deal

Key Features

🎯 Smart Matching

Our algorithm connects you with collectors based on preferred brands, sizes, and budget. We even support split sizes for people with different-sized feet.

📍 Location-Based Discovery

Find sneaker enthusiasts near you. No more searching through endless online listings — discover local collectors ready to trade.

💬 Real-Time Chat

Connect instantly with matched collectors. Discuss details, negotiate prices, and coordinate meetups all within the app.

📚 Collector Resources

Access authentication guides, size conversion charts, and split-buying guides to become a smarter collector.
