Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

Your privacy matters to us.

ShoeMate is committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

Introduction

ShoeMate ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services (collectively, the "App").

What ShoeMate Does: We connect people who have different shoe sizes for each foot with others who have complementary sizes, enabling them to swap shoes and get the perfect fit.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using ShoeMate, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and password
  • Profile Information: Bio, preferred shoe brands, left foot size, right foot size (including split sizes), and style preferences
  • Shoe Information: Photos and details of shoes you want to swap
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through our chat feature to coordinate swaps
  • Location Information: Your general location to find nearby swap partners (with your permission)

Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, time spent
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate location (only with your consent)
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, pages viewed

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our shoe swapping services
  • Connect you with people nearby who have complementary shoe sizes
  • Match you based on shoe size, brand preferences, and location
  • Facilitate messaging and coordination between swap partners
  • Send push notifications for messages and matches
  • Improve our app and develop new features
  • Ensure safety and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

Location Services

ShoeMate uses location services to help you find swap partners nearby. We collect:

  • Your general location to show you potential matches in your area
  • You can control location permissions in your device settings
  • We never share your exact address with other users
  • You can disable location services at any time (though this may limit matching)

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With Other Users: Your profile information (name, photo, bio, shoe sizes) is visible to potential swap partners. Your exact location is never shared.
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics, messaging infrastructure)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

Your Privacy Choices

  • Account Settings: Update your profile, shoe sizes, and privacy settings anytime
  • Location: Enable or disable location sharing in device settings
  • Notifications: Control push notification preferences in the app settings
  • Data Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data at any time

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited access to personal information by authorized personnel only

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time. Some information may be retained for legal, security, or business purposes.

Children's Privacy

ShoeMate is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 16, please contact us immediately.

International Users

ShoeMate is currently available in select regions. If you use our app from outside these regions, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our servers are located.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy in the app
  • Sending a notification to your registered email
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent
