Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 30, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. ShoeMate is committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

Introduction

ShoeMate ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services (collectively, the "App"). ShoeMate connects sneaker collectors to buy, sell, and trade shoes, with a special focus on helping people find mismatched pairs.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using ShoeMate, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and password
  • Profile Information: Bio, preferred shoe brands, sizes (including split sizes), and collecting interests
  • Collection Information: Photos and details of shoes you want to sell or trade
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through our chat feature
  • Location Information: Your general location to find nearby collectors (with your permission)

Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, time spent
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate location (only with your consent)
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, pages viewed

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Connect you with nearby sneaker collectors based on preferences and location
  • Facilitate buying, selling, and trading between users
  • Send push notifications for messages and matches
  • Improve our app and develop new features
  • Ensure safety and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

Location Services

ShoeMate uses location services to help you find collectors nearby. We collect:

  • Your general location to show you collectors in your area
  • You can control location permissions in your device settings
  • We never share your exact location with other users
  • You can disable location services at any time, though some features may not work

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With Other Users: Your profile information, collection photos, and shoe sizes are visible to matched collectors
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

Your Privacy Choices

  • Account Settings: Update your profile, preferences, and privacy settings anytime
  • Location: Enable or disable location sharing in device settings
  • Notifications: Control push notification preferences in the app settings
  • Data Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data at any time

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Regular security assessments
  • Limited access to personal information by authorized personnel only

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time. Some information may be retained for legal, security, or business purposes.

Children's Privacy

ShoeMate is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

International Users

ShoeMate is currently available in select regions. If you use our app from outside these regions, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our servers are located.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy in the app
  • Sending a notification to your registered email
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent
