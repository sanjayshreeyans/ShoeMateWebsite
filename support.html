Frequently Asked Questions

What is ShoeMate?

ShoeMate is a location-based app that connects sneaker collectors to buy, sell, and trade shoes. We specialize in helping people find mismatched pairs - perfect for those who have different shoe sizes for each foot!

How do I find collectors with mismatched sizes?

During signup, you can specify split sizes (e.g., Left: 10, Right: 11). Our matching algorithm will connect you with collectors who have the complementary sizes. You can also filter by split sizes in the discovery settings.

Is ShoeMate free to use?

ShoeMate is a one-time purchase of $4.99 with no subscription fees. This gives you full access to all features including unlimited swipes, messaging, and split-size matching.

How do I stay safe when meeting collectors?

Always meet in public places like malls, sneaker stores, or coffee shops. Bring a friend when possible, and let someone know where you're going. Trust your instincts - if something feels off, don't proceed with the trade.

How do I verify authentic sneakers?

Check out our Resources tab in the app for detailed authentication guides. We recommend:

  • Examining stitching quality and patterns
  • Checking the box label and materials
  • Verifying the shoe's unique details against official images
  • Using professional authentication services for high-value items

Can I change my shoe sizes after signup?

Yes! Go to Profile → Edit Profile → Shoe Sizes. You can update your regular size or split sizes anytime. This will update your matching preferences.

How do I report a problem with another user?

Go to the user's profile, tap the menu icon, and select "Report." You can also block users from their profile. For serious issues, email us at support@shoemate.app.

Why isn't my location working?

Make sure location services are enabled for ShoeMate in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Location Services → ShoeMate). You may need to restart the app after changing permissions.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your data from our servers. Note: This action cannot be undone.

Getting Started

1. Set Up Your Profile

  • Upload a clear profile photo
  • Add a bio describing your collecting interests
  • Specify your shoe sizes (including split sizes if needed)
  • Select your favorite brands

2. Discover Collectors

  • Swipe through profiles in your area
  • Filter by brand, size, and price range
  • Tap the info button to see more details
  • Swipe right to like, left to pass

3. Connect and Trade

  • When you match, start chatting immediately
  • Discuss details, condition, and price
  • Agree on a safe public meeting place
  • Complete your trade!

Troubleshooting

App Crashes

If the app crashes:

  • Force close and reopen the app
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store
  • Restart your device
  • If issues persist, contact support

Can't Send Messages

If messaging isn't working:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Ensure you have an active match
  • Try force-closing and reopening the app
  • Check notification settings

Location Not Updating

If your location isn't showing correctly:

  • Verify location permissions are enabled
  • Enable Wi-Fi for better accuracy
  • Move to an area with better GPS signal
  • Try toggling Airplane Mode on and off

Safety Guidelines

Meeting in Person

  • Public Places Only: Meet in malls, sneaker stores, or busy public areas
  • Bring a Friend: When possible, have someone accompany you
  • Tell Someone: Let a friend or family member know where you're going
  • Daytime Meetings: Prefer daylight hours for visibility and safety
  • Cash or Verified Payment: Use secure payment methods
  • Inspect Before Paying: Always check the shoes before completing the transaction

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Unwillingness to meet in public
  • Pressure to complete the deal quickly
  • Requests for payment before meeting
  • Refusal to show detailed photos
  • Prices that seem too good to be true
  • Aggressive or pushy behavior
